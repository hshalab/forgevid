These fonts render ForgeVid's burned-in captions and lower-thirds. They are
not served as webfonts; they ship here so the ffmpeg drawtext/libass render
path finds a professional display face on every platform (the previous
fallback was whatever system font the host happened to have — DejaVu on
Linux).
